Welcome to the Braivanta Demo Review

Braivanta is a secure, human-verified accessibility workflow platform for schools and VI education teams. It supports Braille work review, Assessment-Safe descriptions, STEM support, pupil records, and approval workflows. The platform uses AI to prepare drafts, but final decisions remain strictly with authorised staff, QTVIs, Braille-literate professionals, and teachers.

How to Review the Demo (10 to 15 Minutes)

  1. 1. Open Demo: Access MVP demo at insighted-ai-mvp.vercel.app/login
  2. 2. Review 5 Features: Test Braille Work Review, Assessment-Safe, STEM Support, Pupil Records, Approvals
  3. 3. Share Feedback: Complete the 10-min form to rate usefulness, pilot interest & pricing preferences
1

Braille Work Review

Upload or capture Braille work to generate an initial English draft. Staff review uncertain areas and send for QTVI verification before sharing with teachers.

2

Assessment-Safe

Prepares accessible descriptions for graphs, diagrams, and visual assessment materials, flagging wording that might reveal answers. Staff approve final suitability.

3

STEM Support

Structured descriptions for complex math, science, technology, engineering materials, circuits, graphs, tables, and labeled apparatus.

4

Pupil Records

Secure central place for authorised staff to manage accessibility records, submitted work, review status, verified outputs, and feedback history.

5

Approvals & Audit Trail

Clear human-verification process recording when work was reviewed, corrected, approved, or returned for action. Creates an audit trail showing who completed each stage and when.

⚠️ Safety & Demo-Use Warning:

Please do not upload real pupil data, confidential school documents, live assessment material, or any personally identifiable information (PII) during demo testing. The demo is for product feedback only. Braivanta supports education professionals; it does not replace QTVIs, Braille-literate staff, teachers, SENCOs, or authorised reviewers.

Deliverable 5: Recommended Implementation Option & Formulas

Fastest implementation route using Google Forms + Sheets + Looker Studio (and Microsoft Forms alternative).

Option A: Google Stack (Recommended)

  1. 1. Create Google Form: Set up the 23 questions from Deliverable 3.

  3. 3. Add Cleaned Data Tab: Apply formulas below for lead scoring & tags.

  1. 1. Create MS Form: Mirror questions in Microsoft Forms.
  2. 2. Connect Excel: Stream results directly into Excel Online table.
  3. 3. Power BI Dashboard: Import Excel table into Power BI desktop for interactive visualization.

Google Sheets Lead Scoring Formula Code

Paste this into Column AA of your Cleaned Data sheet to calculate lead score automatically:

=IF(ISBLANK(A2), "", 
  IF(J2>=4, 2, 0) + 
  IF(OR(R2="Very interested", R2="Strongly interested"), 2, 0) + 
  IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("consider a controlled pilot", S2)), 1, 0) + 
  IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("consider a paid pilot", S2)), 2, 0) + 
  IF(OR(T2="Yes", ISNUMBER(SEARCH("Possibly", T2))), 2, 0) + 
  IF(OR(U2="£300 per month", U2="£400 per month", U2="More than £400 per month"), 2, 0) + 
  IF(ISNUMBER(SEARCH("Yes", W2)), 2, 0) + 
  IF(X2="Yes", 1, 0) + 
  IF(NOT(ISBLANK(Y2)), 1, 0)
)

Lead Status Categorization Formula

=IF(AA2="", "", IF(AA2>=8, "Hot", IF(AA2>=4, "Warm", "Cold")))
